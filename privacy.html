Privacy Policy

I set up Google Analytics on this site because I wanted to know if anyone was actually reading it. In order to have Google Analytics, you have to have a privacy policy. So here's mine:

  • If you submit feedback through the contact form, I will see the feedback you submitted.
  • If you decide to include your email address, I will see your email address.
  • I don't share any of that information — or any information of any kind — with third parties. I wouldn't even begin to know how to do that.

For more information about how Google Analytics collects data, visit www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/
